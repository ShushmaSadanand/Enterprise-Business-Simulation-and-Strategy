## Project: Strategic Adaptability & Turnaround Operations (Company 07 Simulation)
**File:** `BS_G9_C7_ReportFinal.pdf` (Executive Simulation Reflection Report)

A comprehensive corporate performance audit and strategic summary of **Company 07** throughout an intensive multi-round competitive business simulation. The project documents the operational trajectory of transforming a firm from an initial net loss and negative growth into a highly profitable market leader with an 18% market share.

### Core Business & Simulation Insights
* **The Turnaround Trajectory:** Diagnosed the initial strategic failures characterized by a negative 5% evolution rate and consistent net losses. Formulated adaptive pricing, targeted R&D scheduling, and inventory rebalancing that successfully drove a recovery to a positive 9% evolution rate and consistent net income.
* **Multi-Product Optimization Matrices:** Evaluated individual margins across 4 distinct product lines to optimize real-time factory operations. Identified Product 3 as the high-margin anchor (generating a peak net margin of €6,163) to scale production while aggressively adjusting Product 4's price upwards (€75 to €85) to restore unit profitability without sacrificing essential market share.
* **Capital Allocation & Debt Management Strategy:** Leveraged a stable cash cushion of €16,610 to pivot from aggressive customer-acquisition spending toward balance sheet stabilization—including tactical reductions in low-sensitivity advertising and a proactive repayment blueprint targeting a €44,671 long-term liability to optimize overall corporate solvency.
* **Cross-Functional Retrospective Lessons:** Documented critical executive takeaways highlighting the necessity of assertive early market entry, automated cross-departmental communication loops, and data-driven demand forecasting over reactive, short-sighted pricing strategies.
